Our Story

From Green Bay to All of Northeast Wisconsin

Founded by Jorge Cruz in 2014, Geo Carpentry LLC was built on a simple mission: deliver exceptional craftsmanship with the honesty and integrity that Wisconsin families and businesses deserve.

Over the past decade, we've grown from a small carpentry shop into one of Northeast Wisconsin's most trusted construction companies — serving homeowners from Green Bay to Appleton, Oshkosh, Sheboygan, Manitowoc, and beyond.

Every project we take on — whether it's a single custom cabinet or a full home renovation — gets the same care and attention to detail that earned us our 500+ satisfied clients.
