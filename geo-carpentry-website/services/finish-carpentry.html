What We Do

Finish Carpentry Services

Expert installation of premium pre-made materials. We install — we don't fabricate — so you get factory quality with professional craftsmanship at every joint.

🔨

Crown Molding & Baseboards

Crown molding, baseboards, chair rails, and decorative trim installation that transforms ordinary rooms into elegant spaces. We work with pre-milled materials from trusted Wisconsin suppliers.

🚪

Door & Window Trim

Interior door casings, window trim, and jamb extensions. Clean miter joints, level installation, and perfect reveals — the details that make a house feel finished.

🪵

Hardwood Floor Installation

Oak, maple, hickory, and engineered hardwood installation. Nail-down, glue-down, and floating floor installations — plus refinishing and repair of existing hardwood.

🪜

Stairs & Railings

Interior staircase installation and repair. Tread and riser replacement, railing installation, and newel post repair — built to Wisconsin code.

🚪

Interior Door Installation

Pre-hung and slab door installation. Bifold, French, pocket, and barn doors. Hinges, handles, and strike plates installed clean and level.

🏠

Wall Paneling & Wainscoting

Pre-made wainscoting, beadboard, and wall paneling installation. Accent walls, mudroom panels, and dining room treatments using manufactured materials.
