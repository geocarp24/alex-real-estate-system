Most Wisconsin homeowners assume construction grinds to a halt November through March. The truth is more nuanced — and often, winter is actually a GREAT time to remodel. Here are 5 winter construction myths we hear all the time.

Myth #1: Contractors Don't Work in Winter

Reality: Most legitimate Wisconsin contractors work year-round. Interior work — kitchens, bathrooms, basements, custom carpentry, renovations — happens all winter long. We're busiest in summer, so winter is actually when we can give projects more focused attention.

Myth #2: Winter Construction is More Expensive

Reality: The opposite is often true. Material costs are usually lower in winter (off-peak), and contractors with winter availability often offer better pricing because demand is lower. The exception: foundation work or major exterior projects.

Myth #3: Winter Projects Take Longer

Reality: Interior projects often go FASTER in winter because there are fewer parallel projects pulling on subcontractors (electricians, plumbers). Material delivery is also faster when supply chains aren't strained by summer demand.

Myth #4: Winter Cold Damages Materials

Reality: Modern materials (paint, drywall mud, adhesives) are formulated to work at indoor temperatures year-round. Wood acclimates fine in heated indoor spaces. The only concern is exterior work like concrete and roofing, which we schedule for spring or fall.

Myth #5: Permits Take Forever in Winter

Reality: Wisconsin building departments are LESS busy in winter, so permits often come back faster. Inspections are typically scheduled within 2–5 days instead of the 1–2 week summer wait.
