The Short Answer: Wisconsin Kitchen Remodel Costs in 2026

A kitchen remodel in Northeast Wisconsin in 2026 typically falls into three tiers: Budget refresh ($15,000–$25,000) — paint, hardware, countertops, basic appliances. Mid-range remodel ($30,000–$60,000) — new cabinets, granite or quartz counters, tile backsplash, mid-grade appliances, flooring. Full luxury remodel ($70,000–$150,000+) — custom cabinets, premium stone counters, layout changes, high-end appliances, lighting redesign, hardwood flooring.

What Drives the Price Up or Down?

Five things move your kitchen remodel cost the most: Cabinets are usually 30–40% of the total budget. Stock cabinets cost less than semi-custom, which cost less than fully custom. Countertops range from $40/sqft for basic laminate to $200+/sqft for premium quartzite. Layout changes (moving walls, plumbing, gas lines) can add $5,000–$15,000. Appliances can range from $3,000 for basic packages to $30,000+ for pro-grade. Flooring ranges from $2/sqft (laminate) to $15+/sqft (real hardwood with installation).

Wisconsin-Specific Factors

A few things make remodeling in Northeast Wisconsin unique. Older Green Bay homes (built pre-1960) often need electrical upgrades to meet modern code, which adds $1,500–$5,000. Wisconsin's freeze-thaw cycles mean exterior walls in older kitchens may have insulation issues that get exposed during demo. And if you're in a historic neighborhood, you may face additional permit requirements.
