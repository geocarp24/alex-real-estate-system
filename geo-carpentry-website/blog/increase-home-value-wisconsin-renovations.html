If you're renovating with resale in mind — or just want to make sure your money is well-spent — not all home improvements are created equal. Here are the 7 renovations that consistently give Wisconsin homeowners the best bang for their buck in 2026.

1. Kitchen Remodel (70–80% ROI)

A mid-range kitchen remodel ($30K–$60K) typically returns 70–80% of its cost in Wisconsin home value. Key: don't over-improve for your neighborhood. A $100K luxury kitchen in a $250K house won't pay off.

2. Bathroom Addition (60–75% ROI)

Adding a second or third bathroom to a Wisconsin home (especially older homes with only one) can return 60–75% of cost. Even better ROI than a full bathroom remodel.

3. Master Suite Addition (60–70% ROI)

A new master suite (bedroom + bathroom + closet) typically returns 60–70% in resale and significantly increases buyer interest in Wisconsin's competitive market.

4. Deck Addition (65–75% ROI)

A new deck — especially composite — adds usable outdoor living space that Wisconsin buyers love. Composite decks return slightly higher than wood because of lower long-term maintenance.

5. Garage Door Replacement (90%+ ROI)

One of the highest-ROI projects nationally — and especially in Wisconsin where curb appeal matters. New insulated garage doors return 90%+ of cost.

6. Basement Finishing (65–75% ROI)

Finishing a basement adds usable square footage at lower cost per sqft than additions. Wisconsin buyers value finished basements highly because of our long winters.

7. Energy-Efficient Window Replacement (65–75% ROI)

New double-pane or triple-pane windows return 65–75% of cost AND save on heating bills — important in Wisconsin where heating costs are significant.
