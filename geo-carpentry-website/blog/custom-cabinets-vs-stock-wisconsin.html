If you're remodeling a kitchen in Northeast Wisconsin, you'll quickly hit a fork in the road: stock cabinets, semi-custom, or fully custom? The price gap is huge — and the quality gap can be even bigger. Here's the honest breakdown from a Wisconsin custom cabinet maker.

Stock Cabinets: Cheapest, Fastest, Most Limited

Cost: $80–$200 per linear foot. Lead time: 1–2 weeks (often in stock). Pros: Cheapest option, immediately available at big-box stores like Menards or Home Depot, easy to install. Cons: Limited size options (only standard 3-inch increments), limited finish choices, often particle board with thin laminate, won't fit non-standard kitchen layouts. Best for: rental properties, tight budgets, or kitchens with standard dimensions.

Semi-Custom: The Middle Ground

Cost: $200–$500 per linear foot. Lead time: 4–8 weeks. Pros: More size flexibility (1-inch increments), more door styles and finishes, better-quality plywood boxes, soft-close hinges and drawer slides. Cons: Still limited to manufacturer's catalog, longer wait, more expensive than stock. Best for: most Wisconsin homeowners who want quality without going fully custom.

Fully Custom: Maximum Quality and Flexibility

Cost: $500–$1,500+ per linear foot. Lead time: 6–12 weeks. Pros: ANY size, ANY style, ANY finish, premium hardwoods (oak, walnut, cherry, maple), dovetail drawers, lifetime hardware, built to your exact kitchen — including non-standard angles and built-ins. Cons: Most expensive option. Longer wait. Requires a skilled custom carpenter (this is what we do). Best for: forever homes, unusual layouts, kitchens with custom needs (oversized islands, built-in appliances, hidden storage).

Our Honest Take for Wisconsin Homes

If you're staying in your home 5+ years and have a budget over $20,000 for cabinets alone, custom is almost always worth it — both for quality of life AND resale value. Wisconsin homes (especially older ones) often have non-standard wall dimensions where stock cabinets force ugly fillers; custom cabinets fit perfectly.
