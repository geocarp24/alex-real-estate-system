Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about selling your house to Pinnacle Holdings — answered straight, no sales talk.

How fast can I sell my house in Green Bay?

We provide cash offers within 24 hours. Once you accept, we can close in as little as 7 days — or on the date that works best for you.

Do I need to clean or fix anything before selling?

Absolutely not. Pinnacle Holdings buys houses in "as-is" condition. You can even leave unwanted items behind — we handle everything.

Are there any hidden fees or commissions?

No. Unlike traditional sales, there are zero realtor commissions and we cover 100% of the closing costs. The offer we make is the amount you get.

What types of properties do you buy?

We buy single-family homes, multi-family units, and distressed properties throughout Green Bay, Appleton, and the surrounding Wisconsin areas.

What if my house is in probate or has liens?

We have experience dealing with complex situations like probate, tax liens, and foreclosures. We'll work with you to find the best solution.

Do I need to hire a lawyer to sell my house to you?

You're always welcome to have legal representation. We handle the paperwork and use professional title companies to ensure a smooth, legal transaction.

What if I have bad tenants in the property?

No problem. We buy rental properties even with difficult tenants or vacant units. You don't have to worry about evictions.

Is this a scam or a legitimate business?

We are a local, registered Wisconsin investment firm. We provide transparent proof of funds and close through reputable local title companies.

How is selling to Pinnacle different from a Realtor?

A Realtor lists your house and charges 6% commission. We ARE the buyers. We pay cash, charge $0 fees, and buy it as-is today.

Am I obligated to accept your cash offer?

Never. Our offers are 100% no-obligation. You can review the offer, talk it over with family, and decide if it's right for you.

Can I sell my house if I live out of state?

Yes! We specialize in helping out-of-state owners sell Wisconsin properties remotely — without ever having to travel here.

What happens at the closing?

We meet at a local title company (or send a mobile notary), sign the final papers, and you receive your funds via wire transfer or check — same day.
